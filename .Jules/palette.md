## 2024-12-07 - Form Submission Keyboard Accessibility
**Learning:** Using `onsubmit` on `<form>` tags rather than `onclick` on `<button type="button">` is essential for standard HTML form functionality like "Enter to submit" to work in Vanilla JS, which improves keyboard accessibility.
**Action:** Audit dynamic modal structures and form setups in the app to ensure all utilize proper `<form onsubmit>` patterns with `<button type="submit">`.
